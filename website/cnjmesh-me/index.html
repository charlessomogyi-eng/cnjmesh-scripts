CNJ Mesh is a community-built LoRa network for Central and South New Jersey — rooted in the South Brunswick–New Brunswick corridor and reaching south, running Meshtastic and MeshCore side by side. Inexpensive radios, encrypted messages, no cell towers, no internet required, no monthly bill.

First things first
Yes, there is a Central Jersey.

Almost as hotly debated as pork roll vs. Taylor ham — but it exists, and it has its own mesh. We don't actually care what part of Jersey you're from. Central, South, shore, or the PA border: if you can hear us, you're one of us. South Jersey especially — you're family here. The more nodes, the better the mesh.

Two protocols, one community

Get on the mesh

All you need is an inexpensive LoRa radio (Heltec, RAK, LilyGo, and others, starting around $30) and the free app for your phone or computer. No license required. We run both major mesh platforms side by side — pick one, or run both.

Meshtastic

Central & South New Jersey

Open-source mesh messaging over LoRa — encrypted group channels and direct messages, with your phone connected to the node over Bluetooth. This is home for the Central and South New Jersey Meshtastic community, on our regional CentralNJ channel.

MeshCore

Growing across Central NJ

A mesh platform built around smart routing instead of flooding — every user runs a companion node, and dedicated repeaters and room servers carry traffic across the network. Encrypted end to end, with apps on Android, iOS, and desktop.

We're building out MeshCore repeater coverage in Central Jersey, with infrastructure in the Kendall Park area and a live map on CoreScope.

Where the RF reaches

Coverage

The core of the mesh sits in the South Brunswick–North Brunswick–New Brunswick–Milltown–Edison corridor, with solid propagation well beyond it.

  • NortheastReliable paths up toward Jersey City and NYC.
  • NorthwestNodes visible out toward Morristown.
  • WestVisibility into the Lehigh Valley mesh in PA.
  • SouthReaching toward the shore and South Jersey — SJ nodes are very welcome here.
