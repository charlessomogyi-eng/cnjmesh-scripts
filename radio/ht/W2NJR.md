# W2NJR Linked Repeater System - Programming Notes

Source: https://w2njr.net/ (checked 2026-10-24). Re-check the site before relying on
this; repeater details change.

## About

- Started in 1995 as a three-site linked system along the Garden State Parkway; now
  statewide, covering the Atlantic City, New York City and Philadelphia triangle.
- Mostly owned and maintained by K2CB (Eric). W2LI (Tri County Radio Association)
  Murray Hill repeaters are affiliated, linked for nets only.
- Open to all licensed amateurs. No dues, no formal meetings.
- Club license: New Jersey Repeater Association, Manahawkin NJ.
- Priority to mobile operators during drive time; casual users yield to scheduled nets.
