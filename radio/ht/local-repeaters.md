# Local Repeaters and Nets (outside W2NJR)

## K2GE - Raritan Bay Radio Amateurs, Sayreville NJ

Added 2026-10-29. Licensee: Raritan Bay Radio Amateurs (FCC record active, granted
2026-01-29, expires 2036-02-21).

| Band | Output (MHz) | Offset | PL (TX) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VHF | 146.760 | -0.6 MHz | 156.7 Hz | Main machine |
| UHF | 443.200 | +5 MHz | 141.3 Hz | Described as a "UHF link" on the CJTN page; confirm it is open to general use before relying on it |

Frequencies and tones confirmed on two sources (a chat-search summary and the Central
Jersey Traffic Net page at qsl.net/kb2vro/cjtn). Same 141.3 Hz tone as W2NJR on the UHF side.
EchoLink and IRLP access was also mentioned but is unverified.

CHIRP import file: `codeplugs/K2GE_CHIRP.csv`

## Nets heard about on K2GE

- **Middlesex County Chat Group Net** - reported Wednesday 8:30 PM local, and also Sunday,
  Monday, Thursday and Friday at 8:30 PM. UNVERIFIED: this came from a search summary and
  could not be confirmed on any page I could open. Listen in on a Wednesday before relying on it.
- **Central Jersey Traffic Net (CJTN)** - daily at 8:00 PM local on K2GE, an NTS-style
  radiogram net (Section Traffic Manager WB2FTX, Net Manager KB2VRO). Good practice for
  the ARES/traffic goal.
