## Plan

1. Easiest: each end uses its nearest W2NJR repeater; the linked system carries the QSO.
2. Test the middle: from home, load W2NJR Tinton Falls 449.525 (-5, 141.3) and see whether it opens squelch or answers a call.
3. Find out what radio/antenna the Whiting person has and where in Whiting they are.
4. If Tinton Falls is marginal, a better antenna (roll-up J-pole, mag-mount) at home may close the gap.
